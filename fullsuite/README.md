# Complete comparison of implemented methods

One immutable registered protocol and code revision cover four deliberately separate domains. No pooled ranking or invented common labels:

* Basic 13-slot simulation: ten fresh seeds, 200 test runs, 800 episodes. Corrected behavioral training labels; all supervised models fit identical selected rows. Integrity, residual max, Mahalanobis, EWMA, CUSUM, raw/context IF/HGB/MLP, hard-rule OR variants and uniform/oldest/ML schedules.
* Rich 13-slot simulation: ten fresh seeds, 720 matched test runs, 360 episodes across nine families. All prior rich models, hybrids and two calibration units; add a stronger instantaneous structural rival, EWMA and CUSUM. Four statistical/learned priorities plus uniform and oldest receive equal acquisition credits and mandatory coverage, using only previous-tick risk. Zero paired gain for the invisible negative control is required.
* External Granomaly: fixed raw CSV Git blobs, normal-only training/calibration/holdout with 32-row embargo; eight applicable unsupervised methods. HGB/MLP use two separately reported leave-capture-out transfer folds (NRF to SMF and reverse), with no target-capture training. No NFProfile/CU/witness is fabricated. No reproduction of the source paper's TGN.
* Native Open5GS: unmodified official full registration suite and a separate instrumented NRF/AUSF/NSSF study on three runners. Native process stop/resume and pause/resume produce chronological real responses. All applicable availability detector families use the same eight features and fit rows. Continuous authenticated CU sampling and separately signed runtime witnesses provide positive/UNKNOWN/authentication-alarm state; a hard availability/policy/CU alarm cannot be cleared by ML. Native sidecar/host trust limits from cu5g remain. Models are scored retrospectively on causal live observations; no claim of deployed ML remediation or malicious NF exploitation.

All experimental changes are new fullsuite files and one new workflow; prior sources are Git-blob checked. Source-specific historical protocols retain their old seed lists; this experiment's seed selection, corrections and additional methods are governed by fullsuite/protocol.json and its SHA256 in every new summary.

Cross-domain differences are material: HGB/MLP cannot consume absent procedure tokens on packet or liveness datasets; rich features are not real 3GPP semantics. TGN/GNN/LSTM/Transformer are explicitly not implemented and receive no fabricated results. Epoch/label/capture ID/episode truth is excluded from model inputs. No exploit, live semantic attack hook or external target interface is added.

Native CU requests are issued on a fixed 16-second wall-clock period while capture runs. The corresponding nominal 32 ticks is not an actual timing guarantee during timeouts or readiness waits. Witnesses are requested between observations; missing, stale or pending inputs remain UNKNOWN. Accepted cached CUs are not counted as new messages. Native stop/pause labels describe availability only. Signed benign restart controls and offline corruption conditions remain separately reported.

The rich instantaneous rival and new supervised training corrections may reduce prior apparent ML gains. Successful CI requires correct execution, data provenance and invariants; it never requires ML superiority. All warnings and failures are preserved. Empirical 1% calibration is not a guarantee at test time, at episode level or over an indefinite stream. Costs of passive telemetry, full ML inference and all physical channels are not captured by scheduler acquisition credits.
